# SEED reasoning, evidence and composition — CVAA reference

27 November 2026. Research link, not an executable vaccine.

The [SEED reflection](https://github.com/Ventusltd/seed-data/blob/main/10_REASONING_AND_COMPOUNDING_INTELLIGENCE.md) proposes preserving the results of reasoning as verifiable, composable instruments. Functional reasoning is not proof of consciousness, and agent agreement is not an independent verification result. A higher-order SEED earns promotion through its own tests, including refusal and failure cases; measure the reduction in meaningful human decisions against equivalent acceptance criteria. Preserve dependency closure and exact proof, and refuse to equate structural coverage with executable capability. Existing CVAA registry and lock rules remain unchanged.
