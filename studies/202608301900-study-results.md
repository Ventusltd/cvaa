## What changed in the doctrine because of the numbers

1. The sandbox that shipped this morning was not one. Worker threads contain faults, not
   intent. Real containment needs a process, the permission model and a namespace, and even
   then one signal escaped until the pid namespace was added. Claims about sandboxes must be
   accompanied by the attacker list that failed against them.
2. History is the richest context. Adding commits[] moved recall from 0.75 to 0.99 on the
   amnesia class. cvaa is a history linter that happens to read files.
3. Hygiene and amnesia are different sciences. Hygiene checks are opinions and fail
   everyone; they run at warning level until a repo opts in. Amnesia checks are precise
   and fail nobody who has not got the disease, on four foreign repos and fourteen commits.
4. Every fix today was itself a finding: the no-app-copies activation gap, the on-ledger
   history bug, the kill escape. The study found bugs in the vaccine faster than in the repo.
